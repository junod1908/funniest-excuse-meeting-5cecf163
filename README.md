One razor-sharp line that gets you out of a pointless meeting. Entry for the
[make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/c/u-mfnakmal-the-funniest-excuse-for-skipping-a-meeting-5cecf163):

> Can't make it — I'm still in yesterday's meeting. It never ended, it just moved rooms and changed its name.

`main.ts` writes that single line (109 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`.
A head-to-head AI judge compares excuses two at a time and crowns the biggest laugh.
